Veto Fee — Bespoke Teammate Model

Fee = Valuation × (Seat Downgrade + Teammate Effect), floored proportionally, capped at 80% of the driver's real SLF. The teammate effect is fully performance-driven and lock-aware: it compares the projected points of the ally you'd keep by staying vs the ally you'd gain by moving — and an unlocked ally is only discounted toward a reserve driver, so lock state matters far more for a strong ally than a weak one.

🔄 The Trade

🎚️ Tuning (4 levers)

Cap is fixed at 80% of real SLF (the most anyone can be charged). Sensitivity widens the good-vs-bad-seat gap; teammate influence sets how much ally quality moves the fee.

💪 Performance Inputs

Valuation = prize pool re-shared by performance (sizes the fee). Projected points = each driver's remaining-season scoring, interpolated from their average finishing position (drives the teammate effect).

🔍 Selected Veto Breakdown

TeamStrength = banked championship pts + projected remaining pts of that team's current roster
SeatGap = ((curStrength − destStrength) / strengthSpread) ^ (1/sens)
MateEffect = −mateInfluence × (effDestAlly − effCurAlly) / topProj  (unlocked ally discounted toward a reserve driver)
Fee = clamp( floor , Valuation × (SeatGap + MateEffect) , 80% × real SLF )

🏁 How the Veto Fee Works — For Drivers & Constructors
Fee = Your Value × How Bad the Move Is

Your value sets the ceiling. How bad the move is decides what slice of that ceiling you actually pay.

1 · Your Value

Based on how well you're actually performing this season — points, finishes, podiums — not your salary. Drive well and your value (and veto ceiling) is higher.

2 · The Seat Downgrade

How far the destination team sits below your current team — measured on banked points plus each team's projected remaining points, so it reflects the drivers actually in those cars right now:

  • Similar or better team → cheap veto (you'd want to go).
  • Much weaker team → expensive veto (you're dodging a disaster).
  • If a team is gutted by an earlier trade, leaving it becomes cheaper in later rounds.

3 · The Teammate Effect

Your teammate is your ally — you win the Constructors' together:

  • Stronger teammate at the new team → lowers your fee.
  • If that teammate is locked in → lowers it even more.
  • If they're unlocked (might leave) → smaller discount.
Floor

There's always a small minimum — the basic "right to say no."

Ceiling

You can never pay more than 80% of your SLF (your max championship payout). The fee can never exceed what you could realistically win back.

  • Forced to the worst team? You pay near your ceiling — loyalty is taken seriously.
  • Offered a sideways or better move? Almost free — why block an upgrade?
  • Joining a stronger teammate? Cheaper — the move helps you, so refusing costs less.
  • A backmarker offered a top seat? Barely anything — you'd want to go.
In one sentence: your veto costs more the better you're driving and the worse the team you're sent to — but it's discounted if you'd join a stronger teammate, and it can never exceed 80% of your prize money.
