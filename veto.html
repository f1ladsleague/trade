Veto Fee — Bespoke Teammate Model

Fee = Valuation × (Seat Downgrade + Teammate Effect), floored proportionally, capped at 80% of the driver's real SLF. The teammate effect is fully performance-driven: it compares the projected points of the ally you'd keep by staying vs the ally you'd gain by moving — a stronger destination ally lowers your fee (a locked one lowers it further).

🔄 The Trade

🎚️ Tuning (4 levers)

Cap is fixed at 80% of real SLF (the most anyone can be charged). Sensitivity widens the good-vs-bad-seat gap; teammate influence sets how much ally quality moves the fee.

💪 Performance Inputs

Valuation = prize pool re-shared by performance (sizes the fee). Projected points = each driver's remaining-season scoring (drives the teammate effect).

🔍 Selected Veto Breakdown

SeatGap = ((curPts − destPts) / spread) ^ (1/sens)
MateEffect = −mateInfluence × (effDestAlly − effCurAlly) / topProj  (ally proj discounted if UNLOCKED)
Fee = clamp( floor , Valuation × (SeatGap + MateEffect) , 80% × real SLF )
